## 1. JEPA4R: Embodied JEPA World Models with 4D Point Planning

- 作者：Jiarui Dong, Zhiheng Liang, Yanjun Li, Zeyu Zhang, Heejeong Nam, Haoyu Chen, Hao Tang

- 来源：arXiv

- 发布时间：2026-11-15T03:51:21Z

- 论文链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19771v1

- PDF 链接：https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.19771v1.pdf

- 分类：cs.RO, cs.CV



Joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA) world models predict future embeddings instead of pixels and plan in latent space. Two challenges remain: 2D images leave the metric geometry that manipulation acts on implicit and entangled with appearance, and what these models imagine stays hidden, as they are mostly judged in simulation by short-horizon latent planning. We present JEPA4R, a JEPA world model that observes single-depth-camera point clouds, predicts and plans in their latent space, and acts through a policy head on its frozen latents; we call this 4D point planning. A decoder on the frozen world model shows what it imagines as 4D point clouds, with a mean point error within 1.1 cm over 200 steps. Under the policy-learning protocols of OGBench and CortexBench, JEPA4R leads pixel JEPA models on OGBench Cube-Single, Cube-Double, Cube-Triple and Scene, and ranks first on MetaWorld and Adroit Relocate with 98.4% and 88.0%, above the runner-up, R3M, at 96.0% and 65.3%. Trained only in a digital twin, JEPA4R and its policy heads run zero-shot on the real arm with both a gripper and a dexterous hand.

## 2. ManiLadder: Benchmarking Robot Manipulation Through a Categorized and Multi-Difficulty Level Task Ladder

- 分类：cs.RO



Recent advances in robotic manipulation have enabled increasingly complex and dexterous behaviors, yet existing benchmarks still focus on simple pick-and-place tasks and lack a systematic framework for measuring progress in robot learning along the axis of task difficulty. We present ManiLadder, a large-scale and organized simulation benchmark for robotic manipulation. ManiLadder contains 112 carefully designed tasks stratified into 4 difficulty levels, with 8 categories in each level, spanning rigid-body and deformable-object manipulation, single-arm and dual-arm settings, and gripper and dexterous-hand end-effectors. ## 3. GAP: Generalized Adaptation Prior for Dexterous Piano Playing

- 作者：Jinyi Chen, Hongyi Li, Lei Jiang, Xianwei Liu, Yanhong Liang, Yulin Chen, Yucheng Tao, Yongbin Jin, Hongtao Wang

- 来源：arXiv

- 发布时间：2026-11-09T09:24:46Z

- 论文链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12111v1

- PDF 链接：https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12111v1.pdf

- 分类：cs.RO



Dexterous hands enable versatile manipulation, but policies trained in simulation often degrade on hardware owing to non-idealities, and the high-dimensional action space makes real-world adaptation costly. Piano playing, which requires precise and coordinated multifinger motion, offers a representative testbed. We propose GAP (Generalized Adaptation Prior for Dexterous Piano Playing), a sample-efficient adaptation framework that learns how to adapt, rather than how to act, during simulation pretraining and transfers this adaptation prior to hardware. Between repeated executions, GAP uses feedback to adjust the actions of the next one: a gradient guidance network learns from paired executions how adjusting actions affects playing performance, and its gradients guide these adjustments. In simulation, GAP yields approximately 2.4 times the F1 scores improvement of the existing fast-adaptation baseline for dexterous piano playing under the same interaction budget. The learned prior also generalizes to musical pieces held out from pretraining. On a physical dexterous hand, the same simulation-pretrained prior improves F1 scores by 3.6%-23% across three pieces of varying difficulty after approximately 7 min of adaptation each. These results suggest a practical route to reduce the real-world interaction needed to deploy dexterous hands.

## 4. Porosification of the dielectric layer in alternating-current electroluminescence devices enables high-resolution tactile imaging

- 作者：Mingwei Gu, Licheng Li, Zeyuan Zhou, Ying Yang, Zixiang Nie, Xingyu Ma, Junshuai Chen, Lin Zheng, Jinhui Gu, Jia-Sen Xie, Ziling Wei, Mengxing Huang, Jin Ge

- 来源：arXiv

- 发布时间：2026-11-14T12:47:44Z

- 论文链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19384v1

- PDF 链接：https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.19384v1.pdf

- 分类：cond-mat.mtrl-sci, physics.optics



Vision-based tactile sensing enables high-resolution contact perception, yet bulky optical architectures consume valuable space and constrain the functional design of dexterous robotic hands. Here we introduce a self-emissive tactile imaging strategy based on alternating-current electroluminescence (ACEL) devices, in which a compressible porous dielectric encodes contact pressure into spatial emission patterns without external illumination or discrete sensing pixels. We identify a nonlinear capacitance-dependent emission window that provides a basis for tunable pressure-to-light conversion. Pore-size engineering further suppresses local emission deficiencies, reducing spatial intensity variation by nearly ninefold and enabling faithful imaging of contact features down to 100 μm. The continuous electroluminescent interface also reproduces complex contact patterns without computational reconstruction of electrical fields. Direct lamination of the ACEL device onto a complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor (CMOS) image sensor is expected to enable compact contact imaging in an assembly less than 5 mm thick, substantially thinner than conventional vision-based tactile sensors. This approach could reduce the space required for high-resolution tactile imaging, offering greater design freedom for dexterous robotic hands.

## 7. MetricHand: Geometry-Aware Flow Matching for 4D Hand Reconstruction in Metric World Space

- PDF 链接：https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.21060v1.pdf

- 分类：cs.CV



Egocentric hand motion provides physical motion supervision for dexterous manipulation and vision-language-action pretraining. Reliable use of this supervision requires accurate and temporally coherent 4D hand reconstruction in metric world space. Existing methods typically lift framewise estimates produced under weak perspective into world coordinates. By ignoring joint-dependent depth variation at close range, this approximation couples errors in translation, orientation, and articulation that persist after world lifting and worsen under occlusion. We present MetricHand, a geometry-aware flow matching framework for 4D hand reconstruction in metric world space. MetricHand encodes depth priors from hand-object interaction regions into joint-associated representations that preserve local spatial structure and metric scale. World-space acceleration error decreases by 44.2% and 64.3%, demonstrating improved temporal fidelity alongside more accurate absolute localization.

- 分类：cs.CV



Dense hand contact estimation from a single RGB image is a foundational primitive for dexterous manipulation and 3D interaction analysis. Direct predictors typically decode the entire per-vertex contact map from one shared query, leaving individual vertices ungrounded in the image and smearing contact across adjacent, non-touching surfaces. We present VACO, a vertex-aligned decoder in which each MANO vertex samples local image features at its projected location. ## 14. POKE: Probe Offline, Keep Efficiently for Vision-Language-Action Models

- 作者：Jaehyuk Lee, Yeajin Lee, Suhyun Bae, Donghun Lee

- 来源：arXiv

- 发布时间：2026-11-16T12:52:36Z

- 论文链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21033v1

- PDF 链接：https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.21033v1.pdf

- 分类：cs.CV



Visual token pruning reduces the inference cost of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, but attention-based scores do not directly measure a token's effect on the predicted action. We define action sensitivity as the magnitude of the first-order action change when a token is replaced by its camera's mean token and estimate it for all tokens with a single backward pass. Computing it online is costly: scoring on the full (unpruned) graph needs an extra forward pass at every control step. Scoring on the pruned forward pass is cheaper but causes scoring-graph lock-in: removed tokens lose their direct paths through later layers and tend to receive lower scores, making those locations less likely to return. On replayed observations, even with a random conditioning mask, pruned-graph scoring re-selects 59% of the mask's tokens, versus 6% expected by chance; attention histories show a similar bias. POKE (Probe Offline, Keep Efficiently) computes full-graph action sensitivities offline and distills them into a student with under 0.3M parameters. At inference, the student scores the current observation without backpropagation or previous masks, and score-guided diversity selection retains the tokens. Keeping 32 of 512 visual tokens on LIBERO, POKE achieves 95.2% success on OpenVLA-OFT and 90.8% on $π_{0.5}$ at $1.85\times$ and $1.26\times$ speedups, outperforming attention-, diversity-, and distillation-based pruners, with larger margins on LIBERO-Plus.

- 作者：Zuo Gou, Chenhao Lu

- 来源：arXiv

- 发布时间：2026-11-05T15:05:07Z

- 论文链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08144v1

- PDF 链接：https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.08144v1.pdf

- 分类：cs.RO, cs.LG, cs.SE



Robot reinforcement learning increasingly runs environments outside the trainer: in another process, on another machine, in another language. Many faults at this boundary raise no error. We make the boundary a wire protocol and ask what notices a fault in it.   On Pendulum, HalfCheetah, Hopper and a 7-joint arm (Pusher), we injected up to 23 faults into what crosses the wire and 2 into the trainer's log, from one value to every value. ## 17. Event2Reward: Learning Robotic Rewards from Progress Relations

- 作者：Junnan Nie, Zitian Fan, Jiachen Zhang, Junyi Lao, Likui Zhang, Chenghao Liu, Xiaoyang Lu, Yunbo Wang, Yixue Zhang, Linbo Zhai, Jiayi Li, Mengzhe Wang, Xichang Wu, Bin Wang, Chang Liu, Tianle Zhang, Songfang Huang

- 来源：arXiv

- 发布时间：2026-10-29T02:34:14Z

- 论文链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39212v1

- PDF 链接：https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39212v1.pdf

- 分类：cs.RO, cs.LG



Learning task progress from robot demonstrations provides a practical way to construct dense shaping rewards for robotic reinforcement learning. A common approach uses normalized demonstration time as progress labels, implicitly assuming monotonically increasing task progress. During policy exploration, however, actual task progress can stall or even regress. Such models may mistake regressions for progress, assigning positive shaping rewards to actions that cause progress loss. Real suboptimal and failed executions offer corrective experience, but assigning absolute progress values to their states is difficult. Our key observation is that stalls, regressions, and recoveries establish local relative progress relations even when absolute progress is unknown. We introduce Event2Reward, which uses normalized temporal labels to anchor the progress scale and these within-trajectory relations to constrain local changes, combining absolute and relative progress supervision. Its progress predictions serve as potentials for reward shaping. We curate an event-annotated dataset from approximately 100K raw real-robot execution records, covering successful, suboptimal, and failed executions, without requiring framewise absolute progress labels. Under the same training budgets, Event2Reward improves average policy success by 18.16 percentage points over a baseline using only sparse task rewards across 40 tasks in four LIBERO suites. On real robots, average success after 30 minutes of wall-clock training reaches 60.0% on three precise insertion tasks and 90.0% on three object manipulation tasks, exceeding the sparse-reward baseline under the same budget by 36.7 and 23.3 percentage points, respectively. Ablation studies further show that absolute progress supervision and relative progress relations are complementary, with joint supervision yielding better downstream policy learning.

## 18. A new anthropomorphic robotic hand

- 作者：Immanuel Gaiser, Stefan Schulz, Artem Kargov, Heinrich Klosek, Alexander Bierbaum, Christian Pylatiuk, Reinhold Oberle, Tino Werner, Tamim Asfour, Georg Bretthauer, Rüdiger Dillmann

- 来源：OpenReview

- 发布时间：2008-01-01T00:00:00Z

- PDF 链接：https://openreview.net/pdf?id=qF7gyHc5Z3

- 分类：Humanoids 2008



This paper presents the new robotic FRH-4 hand. The FRH-4 hand constitutes a new hybrid concept of an anthropomorphic five fingered hand and a three jaw robotic gripper. The hand has a humanoid appearance while maintaining the precision of a robotic gripper. Since it is actuated with flexible fluidic actuators, it exhibits an excellent power to weight ratio. These elastic actuators also ensure that the hand is safe for interacting with humans. In order to fully control the joints, it is equipped with position sensors on all of the 11 joints. The hand is also fitted with tactile sensors based on cursor navigation sensor elements, which allows it to have grasping feedback and the ability for exploration.

## 19. DexUMI: Using Human Hand as the Universal Manipulation Interface for Dexterous Manipulation

- 作者：Mengda Xu, Han Zhang, Yifan Hou, Zhenjia Xu, Linxi Fan, Manuela Veloso, Shuran Song

- 来源：OpenReview

- 发布时间：2025-09-19T19:43:41Z

- 论文链接：https://openreview.net/forum?id=EkigXMH9Ik

- PDF 链接：https://openreview.net/pdf?id=EkigXMH9Ik

- 分类：CoRL 2025 Workshop Dexterous Manipulation Spotlight, Dexterous Manipulation, Learning from Human, Imitation Learning



We present DexUMI - a data collection and policy learning framework that uses the human hand as the natural interface to transfer dexterous manipulation skills to various robot hands. DexUMI incorporates hardware and software adaptations to minimize the embodiment gap between the human hand and various robot hands. The hardware adaptation bridges the kinematics gap with a wearable hand exoskeleton. It allows direct haptic feedback in manipulation data collection and adapts human motion to feasible robot hand motion. Our software adaptation bridges the visual gap by replacing the human hand in video data with high-fidelity robot hand inpainting. We demonstrate DexUMI's capabilities through comprehensive real-world experiments on two different dexterous robot hand hardware platforms, achieving an average task success rate of 86%.

## 20. Arm Robot: AR-Enhanced Embodied Control and Visualization for Intuitive Robot Arm Manipulation

- 作者：Siyou Pei, Alexander Chen, Ronak Kaoshik, Ruofei Du, Yang Zhang

- 来源：OpenReview

- 发布时间：2024-01-01T00:00:00Z

- 论文链接：https://openreview.net/forum?id=iyTHgxFL7B

- PDF 链接：https://openreview.net/pdf?id=iyTHgxFL7B

- 分类：CoRR 2024



Embodied interaction has been introduced to human-robot interaction (HRI) as a type of teleoperation, in which users control robot arms with bodily action via handheld controllers or haptic gloves. Embodied teleoperation has made robot control intuitive to non-technical users, but differences between humans' and robots' capabilities \eg ranges of motion and response time, remain challenging. In response, we present Arm Robot, an embodied robot arm teleoperation system that helps users tackle human-robot discrepancies. Specifically, Arm Robot (1) includes AR visualization as real-time feedback on temporal and spatial discrepancies, and (2) allows users to change observing perspectives and expand action space. We conducted a user study (N=18) to investigate the usability of the Arm Robot and learn how users perceive the embodiment. Our results show users could use Arm Robot's features to effectively control the robot arm, providing insights for continued work in embodied HRI.
